# Dalu food support 2026

Due to an ongoing draught in Tana River County, we have decided to again support the secondary school in Dalu with food for this semester.

![Dalu ](/assets/img/blog/Dalu_2026/2026_Dalu_mealdist.jpg){:.lead width="1920" height="860"}
Child in Dalu receiving porridge.
{:.figure}

In the drought-stricken community, the food children receive at school is often the only meal in a day.

![Food for Dalu](/assets/img/blog/Dalu_2026/2026_Dalu_schoolyard.jpg){:.lead width="1960" height="860"}
Children eating in the school yard.
{:.figure}

Supplying meals in school has drastically increased attendance over the summer, which was plaged by absenteeism in the previous years. Malnutrition is a significant problem for many learners that used to significantly limit their academic attainments.

![Food for Dalu](/assets/img/blog/Dalu_2026/2026_Dalu_withBowl.jpg){:.lead width="1960" height="860"}
Exercise books for all children.
{:.figure}
